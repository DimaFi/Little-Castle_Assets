# Reference tree 02

Rebuilt trunk with curved major limbs and fused voxel junctions; dome-shaped crown; birdhouse, decorative knot recess and hanging lantern. Original assets retained.

Uses supplied Albedo.jpg (sRGB), Roughness.jpg (linear), Normal.jpg (linear) from Mat/Realistic_Bark_Texture_01_2k. Normal convention was not specified by source; check green channel on Unreal import. Textures packed in Blender and copied/embedded during FBX export. AO and displacement intentionally unused pending material validation.

This is a reference interpretation, not an exact reconstruction of hidden geometry. Bark mapping on lateral limbs still needs direction-aware UV refinement. Knot is a decorative recess, not a cut tunnel. Generated crown remains more regular and denser than the concept. No LOD or Unreal performance validation yet. Do not call this forest-ready.
