# Visual direction and generated material provenance

World_Meadows_01.png and World_Highlands_02.png are user-supplied references.
They guide the meadow/hill/scarp/forest/river palette and silhouettes; they
do not authorize copying their complete village layout or adding watermills.

The limestone albedo used by Bridge_Stone_A v002 was created in built-in
image generation mode and then assigned to real mesh geometry. It is a
material texture, not an AI replacement for the bridge preview render.
Saved material: ../../Architecture/Bridge_Stone_A/v002/Textures/T_Bridge_Limestone_BaseColor.png.

Prompt used:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single seamless tileable albedo surface texture for a hand-painted cozy medieval game stone bridge. Produce a square flat orthographic material texture filling entire image edge to edge: warm ivory limestone, creamy pale sand and subtle cool grey mottling, soft broad brushwork, subtle worn mineral variation, rare tiny ochre flecks. Quiet and beautiful storybook painted game material, NOT photorealistic, no high frequency photographic noise. This is the bare stone SURFACE, all bricks, rounded corners, joints and bevels are provided by the actual 3D geometry: absolutely NO brick pattern, NO grout, NO masonry lines, NO outlined rocks, NO objects, NO cracks dividing the image into blocks. No baked directional light, no shadows, no ambient occlusion, no highlights, no perspective, no text, no border, no frame. Uniform low contrast, pale warm limestone colour with controlled cool subtle patches; seamless on all four edges.
